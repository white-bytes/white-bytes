### About Me

I'm a full-stack software engineer based in Riverside, CA, with roughly 4.5 years of professional experience building production systems end-to-end — from serverless backends to embedded firmware. I currently split my time between two ventures I run:

- **[Metis AI Consulting](https://metisai.us)** — I help small businesses integrate AI into their existing workflows, building practical tools that fit into how teams already operate rather than forcing a rebuild.
- **[WayProper](https://wayproper.com)** — A cloud-native music marketplace and stem distribution platform built on Cloudflare-native infrastructure, designed to automate distribution workflows for artists and rights holders.

Outside of client and product work, I spend time on embedded systems and RF/WiFi security research — building hardware-to-cloud pipelines and exploring low-level protocols for their own sake.
